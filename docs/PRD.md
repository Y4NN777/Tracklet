# Tracklet — Product Requirements Document

- **Status:** Draft
- **Date:** 2026-06-29
- **Author:** Y4NN

---

## Problem Statement

A young Burkinabe micro-entrepreneur (pastry chef, freelancer, reseller, tailor, photographer...) never knows if their activity is truly profitable. They manage by feel, mix personal and business finances, forget debts, and make decisions without data. Existing tools don't speak to their reality.

## Target User

- **Age:** 18–30
- **Location:** Burkina Faso, French-speaking West Africa
- **Profile:** Early-stage or growing micro-entrepreneur
- **Device:** Android smartphone (mid-range)
- **Payment methods:** Cash, Orange Money, Moov Money
- **Financial literacy:** Zero formal financial training

## User Goals

- Get clear visibility into their real financial situation on the ground
- Separate personal and business finances without friction
- Learn finance through action, not courses
- Build a track record of seriousness to access financing

## Non-Goals

- Formal accounting / tax compliance
- Multi-employee management / payroll
- Online banking / integrated payments
- Complex financial reporting or audit trails

## Use Cases

**As a micro-entrepreneur, I want to:**
1. Track my money across cash and mobile money wallets so I know what I have at any time
2. Record who owes me money and what I owe others so I don't forget
3. Separate my personal spending from my business so I know if my activity is profitable
4. Log my sales and expenses quickly so I can see my margin
5. Know whether I'm actually making money after all costs
6. Set savings goals for my business or personal life
7. Get simple, contextual financial tips that help me make better decisions
8. Access everything offline since internet is not always available
